Meet Curtis

Charm City Mobile Mechanic is a growing Baltimore-area business built around a straightforward idea: bring practical vehicle repair to the customer and communicate clearly from the first call through the completed job.

When you reach out, you speak with the mechanic doing the work. Curtis will review the vehicle and problem, confirm whether the job can be completed on site, and explain the price before work begins.
