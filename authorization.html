Authorization and responsibility

  • I confirm that I own the vehicle or have authority from its owner to approve the work.
  • I authorize only the work and estimated amount identified above. Additional work or charges require my separate approval.
  • I will provide a lawful, reasonably level, and safe service location with adequate clearance, and will disclose known hazards, vehicle modifications, prior damage, and relevant repair history.
  • I authorize reasonable vehicle movement and a road test when reasonably necessary to diagnose or verify the authorized repair.
  • I understand diagnosis and repair may reveal additional or preexisting problems. I am responsible for loss or damage arising from inaccurate information, undisclosed conditions, customer-supplied parts, unsafe premises, or events outside the mechanic's reasonable control.
  • To the extent permitted by Maryland law, Charm City Mobile Mechanic is not responsible for incidental or consequential loss caused solely by a preexisting defect, an undisclosed condition, or my failure to follow post-service instructions. Nothing here releases liability for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or any responsibility that cannot legally be waived.

Manufacturer Special Policy Adjustment Programs

Federal law requires manufacturers to furnish the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (N.H.T.S.A.) with bulletins describing any defects in their vehicles. You may obtain copies of these bulletins from either the manufacturer or N.H.T.S.A. In addition, certain consumer publications or organizations publish this information, which may be available for a fee or for free.

Customer's Rights

  • You may request a written estimate for repairs costing more than $50.
  • You may not be charged more than 10% above a written estimate without your consent.
  • You are entitled to the return of replaced parts unless the parts must be returned to the manufacturer under a warranty agreement.
  • You may not be charged for repairs you did not originally authorize unless you later consent to them.

Because service is performed at a customer-approved location rather than a traditional shop, ask Curtis about responsibility for the vehicle during service and the extent of applicable insurance coverage.
