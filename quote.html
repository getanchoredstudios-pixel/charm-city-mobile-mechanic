Before You Book

Clear answers to common questions from first-time customers.

Is the first quote free?

Send the quote form or call to describe the problem. Curtis will explain what can be quoted from that information and whether an in-person diagnosis is needed before you commit to work.

Are there travel or diagnostic fees?

Fees depend on the location and the type of diagnosis required. Any applicable charge will be explained before a visit is scheduled.

What areas do you serve?

Charm City Mobile Mechanic serves Baltimore and surrounding areas. Send your ZIP code to confirm availability for your location.

When do I pay?

Payment is due after the agreed work is complete. Do not send payment from this website before Curtis confirms the amount.

What if my repair needs a shop?

Not every repair is a safe fit for mobile service. Curtis will review the problem and let you know whether it can be handled at your location.
